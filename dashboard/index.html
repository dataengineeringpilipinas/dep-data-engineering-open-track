Key insights

The leads are local, not national. No contractor dominates nationally, but in a few provinces one contractor holds 36% to 41% of value.
Costs rose over time. Much of the climb in flagged projects is a cost-level effect, so projects should be compared within the same year.
Region IV-B stands out on cost flags even after the time adjustment, led by Oriental Mindoro.
Next step: check shortlisted projects against DPWH Project DIME and COA reports before drawing conclusions.

Method and limitations

Completed projects only. The outlier flag compares a project with its region (and, for the time-adjusted version, region and completion year) but not project type or size. Contractor names are as recorded, so some are truncated and joint ventures are not split. Provinces under 30 projects are left out of concentration rankings. This page is a static snapshot. Source code, notebooks and the full write-up are in the project repository.
